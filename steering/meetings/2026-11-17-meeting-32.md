## Uncertainty and falsification

It does not establish a language, source reading, mechanism, or translation, and it does not establish that the same pattern holds in ZL3a. The next test should distinguish longer shared prefixes from initial-only matches before claiming a stronger ordering mechanism. Any proposed reading must be predeclared and predict held-out folios; failure on held-out material or dependence on post-hoc segmentation falsifies that reading.
