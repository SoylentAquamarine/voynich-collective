## Goal, evidence standard, and falsification

The language-first goal remains unchanged. A candidate must have independent historical motivation, a fixed glyph/word mapping, consistent readings on held-out passages, and an English translation that survives blind review. Statistical fit, image resemblance, LLM word spotting, or a plausible botanical match is not a reading.

A candidate is rejected or demoted if its mapping changes across passages, fails held-out folios, depends on cherry-picked glosses, or cannot outperform preregistered controls. The pastiche hypothesis must likewise predict unseen text and beat alternative generative controls; a toy generator that reproduces Zipf alone is insufficient.
