## Interpretation

The coupling rule, built specifically to target the held-out edge-prediction criterion, produces a clean monotonic dose-response: mean edge gain rises from -0.0132 bits/boundary at `p_couple=0.00` (0.1/16 positive blocks on average — no signal, as expected when coupling is disabled) through +0.2250 at `p_couple=0.30` to +2.4091 at `p_couple=1.00`, and the edge criterion passes all 20/20 seeds at every non-zero `p_couple` value, with the effect size scaling directly with the coupling strength. Every configuration exceeds both the 0.15 threshold and Voynich's own +0.1871 point estimate by a wide margin once coupling is enabled at all. The negative control confirms the effect is attributable to the coupling rule itself, not an accidental side channel: with `p_couple=0`, edge signal collapses to essentially zero. The ablation (uniform-random internal characters instead of the Latin-trained order-2 model, at the best-performing `p_couple=1.00`) still passes the edge criterion 5/5 with an even higher mean gain (+2.5476) — confirming the internal-structure model contributes nothing to the edge signal, positive or negative, exactly as the design intended.

**This edge-criterion pass is expected, not a discovery** (see "Independent review" below): the idealized six-class coupling channel carries `log2(6) - H(row)` bits of mutual information — 0.251, 0.638, 1.193, 1.978, and 2.585 bits at `p_couple = 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.9, 1.0` respectively — already exceeding the 0.15-bit threshold at the weakest coupling strength tested. The empirical results track this closely. The dose-response pattern and negative-control/ablation behavior remain useful corroboration that the implementation matches its design, but the pass itself should not be read as showing edge-coupling is hard to construct; it was engineered to clear a low bar by a wide margin.

Vocabulary openness is calibrated to land close to Voynich's point estimate (69.6–71.3% across configurations vs. Voynich's 69.7%) and **passes cleanly, 20/20, in every configuration including the negative control** — the `p_reuse` mechanism is fully decoupled from the boundary-coupling rule, as designed. Token-order-share is **not** as uniformly satisfied: it passes in a minority-to-mixed fraction of seeds depending on configuration (6/20 at `p_couple=0.30`, 14/20 at `p_couple=0.50`, 5/20 at `p_couple=0.70`, 12/20 at `p_couple=0.90`, 11/20 at `p_couple=1.00`) — real seed-to-seed variance around the required band's edges, not a clean pass or a clean fail.

**The primary claim still fails on every configuration**, but not on the two criteria this project has repeatedly separated on. Edge prediction and hapax openness — the axis Naibbe, Cardan, and self-citation all failed — are handled easily by a boundary-coupling mechanism built specifically for them, and token-order-share is at least partially satisfiable. What fails universally, in all 125 replicates across every `p_couple` value including the negative control and the ablation, is character entropy (H1/H2) and the learned multi-symbol-unit scale: H1 sits around 4.20–4.68 (required 3.83–4.13), H2 around 3.63–4.52 (required 2.54–2.84), and the BPE dependence-gap minimum lands at **k=0 merges** in every single replicate (required 32 or 64) — the internal-structure model's character-level output has essentially no redundancy that multi-symbol merging can exploit, unlike Voynich's actual glyph-cluster structure. This is a genuinely informative failure shape, the inverse of the three historical mechanisms: Naibbe and Cardan failed primarily on edge/vocabulary while passing some mix of entropy/order; this generator passes edge and vocabulary easily while failing entropy/unit-scale universally. That is a real, if partial, answer to the project's open question: cross-token edge coupling and open vocabulary are **not**, on their own, a hard joint target to construct — a simple, disclosed, non-circular mechanism built specifically for them satisfies both easily, though the edge pass specifically was foreseeable from the coupling rule's channel capacity rather than an empirical surprise (see "Independent review" below). What resists easy construction is jointly hitting those *and* Voynich's specific character-level entropy and multi-symbol-unit compression profile *at the same time*. The three historical mechanisms and this constructive null therefore fail for different underlying reasons, not the same one — which is itself evidence that the six-criterion joint profile is doing real discriminating work, not just restating one easy-to-fail property five different ways.

**This does not identify a mechanism, prove meaninglessness, or reject any hypothesis about Voynichese.** It narrows what a full joint-profile match would require: a generator would need the internal-structure component tuned toward Voynich's own entropy/compression profile specifically (which this design deliberately avoided doing, to keep the coupling result uncontaminated by circularity) in addition to the boundary-coupling mechanism already shown sufficient for edge/vocabulary/order. Building and testing that combined version would need its own preregistration, not a post-hoc parameter change to this one.

## Independent review

This design and its execution were self-reviewed and solo-executed by Claude after ChatGPT remained unresponsive for an extended period (user-authorized; see `logs/2026-09-20-claude-solo-boundary-null-selfreview.md`). ChatGPT returned before this report was finalized and reviewed the merged design (PR #24), returning **challenge; pause execution** — a verdict Claude accepted in full after independent verification (`logs/2026-09-20-skeptic-boundary-state-null-review.md`). Disclosed plainly rather than argued away:

- **The edge-criterion pass was expected, not discovered.** ChatGPT calculated the idealized six-class coupling channel's mutual information analytically (0.251 to 2.585 bits across the `p_couple` sweep, all at or above the 0.15-bit threshold) *before* this execution's results existed. Claude independently reproduced the calculation by hand and confirmed it exactly. The empirical pass reported above is real and correctly implemented, but it does not demonstrate that edge coupling is hard to construct — the design was, by its own channel capacity, close to guaranteed to clear the threshold.
- **The claimed criterion isolation was imprecise.** The design document originally stated the internal-structure step (Latin-trained model, reused Voynich token lengths) "does not target any criterion by construction." ChatGPT correctly pointed out that these choices affect entropy, BPE compressibility, token-order share, and vocabulary growth simultaneously — they are not *deliberately optimized* for any one of them (unlike the coupling rule's deliberate targeting of edge prediction), but the original wording overstated a clean separation that doesn't exist.
- **Several generator implementation details were decided during coding, not frozen in the design document beforehand**: the order-2/order-1/unigram backoff scheme, tie-breaking, and length-one token handling. This is a real process gap relative to the discipline every other mechanism test in this project has held to. Claude does not believe these specific choices were biased, but they were not specified in advance as they should have been.

None of this changes the primary FAIL verdict or the universal entropy/unit-scale failure, which do not depend on the edge-criterion framing. It does mean the "edge and vocabulary are easy to construct" half of this result should be read as confirming a predictable consequence of the design rather than as a standalone empirical finding.

## Provenance and execution

- Design: `methods/boundary-coupled-null-preregistration.md`, drafted 2026-09-20, corrected through solo adversarial self-review and implementation-pilot testing after ChatGPT remained unresponsive for an extended period (user-authorized solo execution; see `logs/2026-09-20-claude-solo-boundary-null-selfreview.md`).
- Alphabet: 25 single EVA letter-characters observed in the real Voynich token stream (`a`-`z` minus `w`), partitioned into 6 classes by alphabetical round-robin.
- Internal-structure model: order-2 character Markov model trained on the already-pinned Latin ITTB treebank (`github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Latin-ITTB`), mapped onto the EVA alphabet by the same arbitrary alphabetical ordering used for the class partition (not frequency rank, closing a circularity leak found in self-review).
- Vocabulary mechanism: `p_reuse = 0.87`, calibrated via a self-consistency-only pilot (never compared to Voynich's actual joint profile at pilot time) to land near Voynich's aggregate hapax share.
- Project profile pipeline: `voynich-units` commit `956a7c4fc39981f4d116fa3f4edfccce6d065571`'s `reproduce_naibbe_control.battery()` plus the same held-out edge-prediction test used for Naibbe, Cardan, and self-citation, run unmodified.
- Seeds: 20 fixed seeds (42 + 137*i) for every primary configuration and the negative control, per protocol; 5 seeds for the internal-model ablation (targeting the primary configuration with highest mean edge gain, `p_couple=1.00`).

ChatGPT has since reviewed the design (see "Independent review" above) and returned challenge on three points, all accepted and disclosed. A knowledge-base entry recording this result, incorporating that review, is proposed in a follow-up PR.
