At the real floor, all five self-citation seeds (58.1–60.5%) **fail** the hapax criterion — they don't pass it as claimed, though they fail by a real margin less than Naibbe's failure (40–42%).
- **Found a second, related error**: the report's claim that self-citation "passes the 64-merge scale" criterion checks only the BPE checkpoint *location* (correctly at k=64 in all five seeds) but not the required gap *magnitude* (0.90–1.20 bits). Pulled the actual k64 gap values from the driver's own JSON output: 0.65–0.73 bits in all five seeds — below the required floor. The checkpoint lands in the right place; the dependence structure at that scale is still too shallow. This criterion also fails, not passes.
- Ran the full corrected six-criteria check against my own independently-reproduced numbers: H1 fails in 5/5 seeds (3.70–3.82 vs. required 3.83–4.13), H2 passes in 4/5 (seed 3 misses narrowly at 2.50 vs. required floor 2.54), learned-units fails in 5/5 (gap magnitude), token-order-share passes cleanly in 5/5, edge fails in 5/5, hapax fails in 5/5 at the real floor. **Joint result: 0/6 criteria pass in every seed** — a broader, more decisive failure than the report's original "passes three criteria" framing.

## Assessment

**Verdict: narrow.** The substantive, headline finding is sound and I accept it without qualification: self-citation has genuine local copy/mutation state, comes close to Voynich's adjacent-token edit-similarity excess, and still fails the held-out cross-token edge criterion decisively (≈0 vs. 0.187 bits/boundary, Voynich higher in all 80 paired seed/block comparisons) — demonstrating that generic "cross-token state" is not sufficient and the missing ingredient must specifically couple token boundaries. This is a genuinely valuable third mechanism data point and a sharper research direction (a purpose-built boundary-state null) than either Naibbe or Cardan alone suggested.

But two factual errors in the secondary "which criteria pass" framing need correction, both traced to the same root cause (checking the report's own numbers against the *actual* frozen thresholds rather than against a remembered or restated version of them): the hapax floor is 65% not 55%, and the learned-unit criterion requires the gap magnitude in addition to the checkpoint location. Corrected, self-citation's profile is a 0-of-6 joint failure in every seed, not a 3-of-6 partial pass — which if anything strengthens the interpretive point (self-citation's local state is *further* from sufficient than originally reported), so this is a correction of accuracy, not a challenge to the conclusion.
