**Primary verdict: FAIL** — 0/20 primary replicates pass all six criteria jointly (required >=16/20 for PASS).

Full breakdown and interpretation in `data/derived/external-boundary-state-null-audit-report.md`. Summary: the two new operations (boundary coupling, novelty injection) are compositionally near-independent on the criteria they were each built to satisfy (edge, hapax) but sharply asymmetric on the two criteria that fail — novelty injection alone accounts for essentially all of the entropy/unit-scale damage (H1/H2 leave the required band, BPE k64-gap collapses from 1.03 in the coupling-only/baseline configurations to ~0.6-0.8 whenever novelty is active), while boundary coupling alone leaves entropy/units almost untouched. This holds across all five sensitivity configurations at different `(beta, nu)` values — not a tuning artifact.

## Assessment

This is the most mechanistically specific result of the five mechanism tests run so far in this project (Naibbe, Cardan, self-citation, BCCN, boundary-state-null). Unlike BCCN, which started from an internal-structure model never built to match Voynich's entropy/unit profile, this design starts from Naibbe — a mechanism that already independently satisfies entropy and unit-scale — and adds exactly the two operations the project's prior four-mechanism history suggested were missing (cross-token boundary coupling, open vocabulary). The manipulation checks confirm both operations individually do what they were designed to do. The joint six-criterion target is still unreachable, and the `edge_only` vs. `novelty_only` decomposition identifies *which* of the two operations is responsible: the length-preserving, uniform-substitution novelty-injection rule specifically increases character-level randomness in a way that costs the multi-symbol-unit compression structure Naibbe alone gets right; boundary coupling does not.

The honest limitation: this only tests one specific novelty mechanism (single-atom substitution, uniform over alternatives, deterministic cyclic search). It does not show that no open-vocabulary mechanism could coexist with Voynich-like entropy — only that this one, disclosed and preregistered, does not. A differently-constructed novelty rule (e.g. one that preserves local bigram statistics of the base stream rather than substituting toward a uniform distribution over unused atoms) was not tested and would need its own preregistration.
