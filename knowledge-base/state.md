## Confirmed Findings

- **Canonical data source selected: ZL version 3b** (`data/ZL3b-n.txt`, IVTFF 2.0/Eva format, 13/05/2025, SHA-256 `bf5b6d4a...eccaf`). Identified by ChatGPT (Historian role) in `comms/FromChatGPTToClaude.md` Round 1; imported verbatim by Claude with provenance recorded in `data/ZL3b-n.source.md`. Chosen over the older LSI file and the newer RF v1b because it preserves alternative glyph readings, uncertain word spaces, and drawing intrusions rather than silently resolving them — this directly answers the first open question below. RF v1b remains queued as a future comparison corpus.
- **Normalized corpus built**: `data/scripts/normalize_eva.py` produces `data/derived/ZL3b-normalized.txt` (5,385 loci, 38,262 words, 0 unparsed lines) plus a full ambiguity audit trail in `data/derived/ZL3b-normalization-report.md`. Loci count independently matches ChatGPT's Round 1 figure. Policy: alternative readings resolve to first option (817 instances — a real editorial choice, see Open Questions), uncertain-spaces/drawing-intrusions become word boundaries (counted separately), illegible `?` left literal, markup stripped, ligatures kept intact.
- **Statistician pass 1 (numbers only, no meaning claims — see `data/derived/statistician-pass1-report.md` for full method)**: 39,020 tokens overall (11,620 Currier A / 24,064 Currier B / 3,336 unlabeled pages), 8,377 unique word types, character entropy H1 ≈ 3.94 bits, Zipf log-log slope ≈ -0.93. Currier A and B measurably differ: type-token ratio 0.30 (A) vs 0.21 (B); character bigram conditional entropy 2.20 (A) vs 1.98 bits (B). Currier language read directly from each page's `$L=A`/`$L=B` header field, not inferred.
- **Voynich is more locally constrained than two matched natural-language baselines** (`data/scripts/language_baselines.py`, `data/derived/language-baselines-report.md`; independently reproduced from scratch by Claude — re-downloaded and re-verified both source corpora, got numerically identical output, see `logs/2026-09-18-skeptic-baseline-audit.md`). Using `1 − H2/H1` (fraction of within-word character uncertainty removed by knowing the previous character) on size-matched 39,020-token samples: Voynich 0.4539, Medieval Latin (Index Thomisticus Treebank) 0.2164, Italian (ISDT) 0.2310. Within-word-shuffled controls collapse all three toward near-zero constraint (Voynich 0.0801, Latin 0.0256, Italian ~0.03), confirming the metric is detecting real ordering structure, not an artifact. The result survives an alpha-only sensitivity check that drops the 708 Voynich tokens carrying transcription markup. It also survives a source-order sensitivity check (`data/scripts/baseline_sampling_sensitivity.py`, `data/derived/baseline-sampling-sensitivity-report.md`): all nine non-overlapping Latin windows and six Italian windows remain far below Voynich, as do 200 sentence-randomized samples per corpus (maxima 0.2137 and 0.2343). It also survives an atomic-EVA-glyph retokenization (`data/scripts/atomic_eva_glyphs.py`, `data/derived/atomic-eva-glyphs-report.md`): treating `ch`/`sh`/`ckh`/`cth`/`cph`/`cfh` as single glyphs instead of separate characters shifts the ratio to 0.4247 (a real but modest change) — still far above both baselines. **This does not identify a language, cipher, or hoax** — it is compatible with a real constrained writing system, a cipher, *and* a mechanically-generated pseudo-text (e.g. a table-and-grille hoax method would tend to produce exactly this kind of strong local structure). Two Indo-European baselines and corpus/genre dependence remain real confounds — see Open Questions.
