## Confirmed Findings

- **Canonical data source selected: ZL version 3b** (`data/ZL3b-n.txt`, IVTFF 2.0/Eva format, 13/05/2025, SHA-256 `bf5b6d4a...eccaf`). Identified by ChatGPT (Historian role) in `comms/FromChatGPTToClaude.md` Round 1; imported verbatim by Claude with provenance recorded in `data/ZL3b-n.source.md`. Chosen over the older LSI file and the newer RF v1b because it preserves alternative glyph readings, uncertain word spaces, and drawing intrusions rather than silently resolving them — this directly answers the first open question below. RF v1b remains queued as a future comparison corpus.
- **Normalized corpus built**: `data/scripts/normalize_eva.py` produces `data/derived/ZL3b-normalized.txt` (5,385 loci, 38,262 words at the comma/period-boundary level, 0 unparsed lines) plus a full ambiguity audit trail in `data/derived/ZL3b-normalization-report.md`. Loci count independently matches ChatGPT's Round 1 figure. Policy: alternative readings resolve to first option (817 instances — this is IVTFF's own documented default, not an arbitrary choice, see Open Questions for the verification), uncertain-spaces/drawing-intrusions/misaligned-intrusions become word boundaries (counted separately: `.`/`,`/`<->`/`<~>`), illegible `?` left literal, markup stripped, ligatures kept intact. **`<~>` fix (2026-09-19)**: 6 occurrences (all `f34r`) were previously stripped with no boundary inserted, silently joining two adjacent words — found by ChatGPT, verified against the primary IVTFF spec by Claude, fixed, and every dependent artifact regenerated and diffed (`logs/2026-09-19-tilde-boundary-fix.md`). Final tokenized corpus is 39,026 tokens (was 39,020) — every downstream headline number below already reflects the fix; the change was tiny everywhere it touched.
- **Statistician pass 1 (numbers only, no meaning claims — see `data/derived/statistician-pass1-report.md` for full method)**: 39,026 tokens overall (11,620 Currier A / 24,070 Currier B / 3,336 unlabeled pages), 8,373 unique word types, character entropy H1 ≈ 3.94 bits, Zipf log-log slope ≈ -0.93. Currier A and B measurably differ when pooled: type-token ratio 0.30 (A) vs 0.21 (B); character bigram conditional entropy 2.20 (A) vs 1.98 bits (B). Currier language read directly from each page's `$L=A`/`$L=B` header field, not inferred. **Correction (see next finding below): the pooled H2 gap does not mean individual B pages are more locally predictable than individual A pages — it's a page-pooling artifact.**
- **Pooled A/B entropy gap is a page-pooling artifact, not per-page predictability** (`data/scripts/currier_metadata_analysis.py`, `data/derived/currier-metadata-report.md`; independently reproduced from scratch by Claude, including the entropy-decomposition math itself — see `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-currier-metadata-audit.md`). Within Herbal pages (the only illustration class with substantial A and B samples), pooled B has lower H2/higher constraint than pooled A — but at the **page level**, mean constraint is statistically indistinguishable: 0.5447 (A) vs 0.5391 (B), 20,000-permutation two-sided p = 0.519. An entropy decomposition (`pooled_H2 − within_page_H2 = I(Next;Page|Prev)`, the standard conditional-mutual-information identity, verified correct) shows pooling adds 0.394 bits of page-to-page heterogeneity to A but only 0.276 bits to B — B's pages are more *mutually uniform* with each other, not more internally predictable. This survives the atomic-EVA-glyph retokenization (gap widens from 0.117 to 0.167 bits under that tokenization). Separately: Currier A/B is far more tightly associated with Davis scribal hand (Cramér's V 0.980, conditional entropy 0.054 bits) than with illustration/topic class (V 0.668, 0.596 bits) among 197 labeled pages — a real production-level association, but the one apparent same-hand/same-illustration control cell fails visual inspection (very different page layouts), so this cannot yet decide between "language," "scribal convention," "exemplar/batch," or some combination as the underlying driver.
- **Voynich is more locally constrained than two matched natural-language baselines** (`data/scripts/language_baselines.py`, `data/derived/language-baselines-report.md`; independently reproduced from scratch by Claude — re-downloaded and re-verified both source corpora, got numerically identical output, see `logs/2026-09-18-skeptic-baseline-audit.md`). Using `1 − H2/H1` (fraction of within-word character uncertainty removed by knowing the previous character) on size-matched 39,026-token samples: Voynich 0.4539, Medieval Latin (Index Thomisticus Treebank) 0.2164, Italian (ISDT) 0.2310. Within-word-shuffled controls collapse all three toward near-zero constraint (Voynich ~0.08, Latin ~0.03, Italian ~0.03), confirming the metric is detecting real ordering structure, not an artifact. The result survives an alpha-only sensitivity check that drops Voynich tokens carrying transcription markup. It also survives a source-order sensitivity check (`data/scripts/baseline_sampling_sensitivity.py`): all non-overlapping Latin/Italian source-order windows and 200 sentence-randomized samples per corpus remain far below Voynich. It also survives an atomic-EVA-glyph retokenization (`data/scripts/atomic_eva_glyphs.py`): treating `ch`/`sh`/`ckh`/`cth`/`cph`/`cfh` as single glyphs instead of separate characters shifts the ratio to 0.4247 (a real but modest change) — still far above both baselines. It also survives the `<~>` word-boundary fix above and a deliberately-adverse last-option alternative-reading stress test (`data/scripts/alternative_reading_sensitivity.py`, `data/derived/alternative-reading-sensitivity-report.md`): changing all 817 uncertain readings to their *least*-likely option moves every headline metric by less than 0.001. **This does not identify a language, cipher, or hoax** — it is compatible with a real constrained writing system, a cipher, *and* a mechanically-generated pseudo-text (e.g. a table-and-grille hoax method would tend to produce exactly this kind of strong local structure). The original two-language comparison is now superseded by the broader panel below, which closes the "Indo-European only" objection; see next finding.
- **The local-constraint gap survives a preregistered, typologically diverse, document-stratified panel** (`data/scripts/document_baseline_panel.py`, `data/derived/document-baseline-panel-report.md`; manifest reviewed and approved *before* any result existed — `logs/2026-09-19-baseline-panel-manifest-review.md` — then results independently reproduced from scratch by Claude, exact match on all 2,000 replicate values, not just headline stats — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-document-panel-audit.md`). Panel: Turkish (agglutinative), Estonian (agglutinative), Arabic (Semitic abjad), Hebrew (Semitic abjad), English (document-rich analytic control) — 200 document-stratified 39,026-token samples per language, ≥11 distinct documents per sample, no document contributing more than 10%. Predeclared, conservative decision rule: **every** corpus's 97.5th-percentile constraint must stay below the atomic-EVA Voynich reference (0.4247) for the claim to survive. **Result: PASS for all five**, with substantial margins — Turkish (closest) 0.2359 upper bound, gap 0.1888; Hebrew (furthest) 0.0968 upper bound, gap 0.3279. Within-token shuffled controls stay near zero for all five, confirming real ordering structure is being measured. Syntactic-token sensitivity view shows a material Arabic/Hebrew shift (UD clitic segmentation matters) but doesn't erase the gap. **Still does not distinguish language, cipher, or pseudo-text** — per `methods/falsification-standard.md`, this remains a measurement, not grounds for an Active Hypothesis. The panel contains no syllabary/logographic script — a real, named limitation of available document-stratified UD data, not an oversight.
- **The 30 Currier-unlabeled pages track diagram layout and Davis hand 4, not damage** (`data/scripts/unlabeled_currier_pages.py`, `data/derived/unlabeled-currier-pages-report.md`; independently reproduced exactly by Claude — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-unlabeled-pages-audit.md` — plus manuscript-image spot-checks against 2 of the 5 committed Wikimedia Commons scans, license verified as genuine public domain). The earlier "likely foldouts/damaged" guess is retired: 26 of 30 unlabeled pages (92.6% of unlabeled tokens) form one continuous hand-4 diagram sequence (`f67r1`–`f73v`), and unlabeled pages are only 12.6% paragraph-text loci vs. 87.1% on labeled pages — dominated instead by label/radial/circular loci. The strongest single case: `f65v` is an intact, undamaged, non-foldout herbal page (visually confirmed) that still lacks a Currier label, ruling out damage as a complete explanation. Best-supported interpretation: a **coverage convention** — Currier labeling mainly covers body-text-rich pages and largely omits diagram-dominated text — not a claim about what language the omitted text is in. **Does not resolve** whether the hand-4 diagram text is linguistically closer to A or B.
- **Voynichese order sits mainly at token edges and a multi-symbol unit scale; whole-token succession is weak but not absent** — the project's first cross-reference to external published research (Rozanova & Temerev, *A Glyph Is Not a Letter, a Token Is Not a Word, a Space Is Not a Space*, arXiv:2608.17096, 17 Aug 2026; public code `github.com/lrozanova/voynich-units`). Both parties independently verified the paper exists and audited its own citation accuracy: the paper cites commit `66f8ada` as its analysed snapshot, but that commit contains zero of the advertised reproduction scripts — the real final bundle is `956a7c4` (one commit later, matching the submission date) — a real defect in the paper's own documentation, not a numerical error. ChatGPT ran the paper's public drivers at the correct commit; Claude independently re-ran them from a fresh, security-reviewed clone (no subprocess/network/eval/pickle calls found anywhere in the repo) and got an exact match on every reported number, including the held-out-quire learned-unit trough (minimum at 32 BPE merges: 1.686→1.423→1.379→1.490 bits at 0/16/32/64 merges) and the 16/16 leave-one-quire-out result at a full-corpus trough of 64 merges. ChatGPT then built `data/scripts/external_edge_crossfit.py`, an independent held-out generalization test (not from the paper) for the paper's cross-token edge-glyph coupling claim; Claude audited both scripts line-by-line for train/test leakage (none found — training statistics are built only from non-held-out quires, held-out glyphs absent from training are explicitly bucketed as unknown rather than leaking through) and independently reproduced its numbers exactly too: a last-glyph→next-first-glyph model trained outside each quire gains 0.174 bits/boundary held out (16/16 quires positive), rising to 0.179 bits when restricted to line-interior boundaries only, with 0.194 excess bits surviving the strictest of three position-preserving permutation-null designs (1,000 shuffles each, observed value exceeding every single permutation). **Qualification (ChatGPT, independently code-audited and numerically reproduced by Claude — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-token-order-sensitivity-audit.md`)**: the paper's own "not in whole tokens" framing is measurably too categorical. A properly nested (leakage-free) leave-one-quire-out test finds a real, if much weaker, whole-token-identity signal: 0.0647 bits/boundary, positive in 15/16 quires (only quire J negative, −0.0143 bits/119 boundaries) — versus 0.174 bits/boundary at token edges. Separately, the paper's fixed 2,000-type vocabulary cap retains very different coverage per corpus (84.27% Voynich vs. 67.46% Latin botanical); at matched 70% coverage the Voynich/Latin-botanical gap nearly vanishes (1.8411% vs. 1.8588%, a 0.0177-percentage-point difference) — so the exact numerical distance to the nearest control is representation-dependent, though Voynich remains at or below every tested control across the full coverage range. The paper's small blind direct-pixel check also reproduces from its archived tables (`data/scripts/external_direct_pixel_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-direct-pixel-report.md`): certain gaps average 4.985 pixels versus 3.143 for uncertain gaps (+1.842), positive on all 5 informative folios and after line matching plus local-height normalization. This is corroboration of the coordinate result, not an independent image study: only 21 uncertain cases survive, extreme thresholds reverse the contrast, and the public bundle omits the frozen manifest, blind-QC file, label key, and Yale scans needed to rerun raw extraction. **The paper's own most discriminating fact**: a published Voynich-imitating cipher and a self-citation generator both reproduce the low entropy, the unit scale, and the weak whole-token order — but **neither reproduces the edge-glyph coupling or the manuscript's open, hapax-rich vocabulary** (70% singleton word types vs. 41% and 59–60% in the two control generators). This is more discriminating than anything purely internal to this project has produced, but it still does not identify meaningful language, cipher, or a specific generative mechanism — see `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-external-paper-audit.md` for the full verification chain.
