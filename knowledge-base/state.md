- **Statistician pass 1 (numbers only, no meaning claims — see `data/derived/statistician-pass1-report.md` for full method)**: 39,026 tokens overall (11,620 Currier A / 24,070 Currier B / 3,336 unlabeled pages), 8,373 unique word types, character entropy H1 ≈ 3.94 bits, Zipf log-log slope ≈ -0.93. - **Pooled A/B entropy gap is a page-pooling artifact, not per-page predictability** (`data/scripts/currier_metadata_analysis.py`, `data/derived/currier-metadata-report.md`; independently reproduced from scratch by Claude, including the entropy-decomposition math itself — see `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-currier-metadata-audit.md`). Within Herbal pages (the only illustration class with substantial A and B samples), pooled B has lower H2/higher constraint than pooled A — but at the **page level**, mean constraint is statistically indistinguishable: 0.5447 (A) vs 0.5391 (B), 20,000-permutation two-sided p = 0.519. An entropy decomposition (`pooled_H2 − within_page_H2 = I(Next;Page|Prev)`, the standard conditional-mutual-information identity, verified correct) shows pooling adds 0.394 bits of page-to-page heterogeneity to A but only 0.276 bits to B — B's pages are more *mutually uniform* with each other, not more internally predictable. This survives the atomic-EVA-glyph retokenization (gap widens from 0.117 to 0.167 bits under that tokenization). Separately: Currier A/B is far more tightly associated with Davis scribal hand (Cramér's V 0.980, conditional entropy 0.054 bits) than with illustration/topic class (V 0.668, 0.596 bits) among 197 labeled pages — a real production-level association, but the one apparent same-hand/same-illustration control cell fails visual inspection (very different page layouts), so this cannot yet decide between "language," "scribal convention," "exemplar/batch," or some combination as the underlying driver.

- **Wholesale Currier A/B imputation for the hand-4 sequence fails a page-held-out proximity test** (`data/scripts/hand4_currier_proximity.py`, `data/derived/hand4-currier-proximity-report.md`; independently reproduced exactly by Claude, including the leave-one-page-out leakage check and the specific illustration/quire gradient claim — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-hand4-proximity-audit.md`). A page-held-out character-bigram log-odds classifier correctly discriminates labeled A/B source pages out of sample (99.1% / 100.0% expected sign), but scored against the 26 hand-4 pages it is **not one stable class**: 11 pages score A-like, 15 score B-like, with a near-boundary target median (-0.0515 bits/n-gram) far closer to zero than either source median (+0.398 A / -0.360 B). Scores decline sharply in manuscript reading order (Spearman ρ=-0.782, 20,000-permutation p=0.00015): every one of the 12 zodiac pages (quires K/L) scores B-like, while the earlier astronomical/cosmological pages (quires I/J) are mixed or A-like — tracking illustration class and quire, not staying flat. **No same-hand/same-topic control exists to separate this from section drift**: the labeled corpus has zero astronomical or zodiac pages and only 4 cosmological pages, all Currier B. **This is not a language-transition finding** — it does not identify what the gradient is, only that treating hand 4 as uniformly A or uniformly B is unsupported. Currier's missing labels for these 26 pages should stay missing, not be imputed.
- **Voynichese order sits mainly at token edges and a multi-symbol unit scale; whole-token succession is weak but not absent** — the project's first cross-reference to external published research (Rozanova & Temerev, *A Glyph Is Not a Letter, a Token Is Not a Word, a Space Is Not a Space*, arXiv:2608.17096, 17 Aug 2026; public code `github.com/lrozanova/voynich-units`). Both parties independently verified the paper exists and audited its own citation accuracy: the paper cites commit `66f8ada` as its analysed snapshot, but that commit contains zero of the advertised reproduction scripts — the real final bundle is `956a7c4` (one commit later, matching the submission date) — a real defect in the paper's own documentation, not a numerical error. ChatGPT ran the paper's public drivers at the correct commit; Claude independently re-ran them from a fresh, security-reviewed clone (no subprocess/network/eval/pickle calls found anywhere in the repo) and got an exact match on every reported number, including the held-out-quire learned-unit trough (minimum at 32 BPE merges: 1.686→1.423→1.379→1.490 bits at 0/16/32/64 merges) and the 16/16 leave-one-quire-out result at a full-corpus trough of 64 merges. ChatGPT then built `data/scripts/external_edge_crossfit.py`, an independent held-out generalization test (not from the paper) for the paper's cross-token edge-glyph coupling claim; Claude audited both scripts line-by-line for train/test leakage (none found — training statistics are built only from non-held-out quires, held-out glyphs absent from training are explicitly bucketed as unknown rather than leaking through) and independently reproduced its numbers exactly too: a last-glyph→next-first-glyph model trained outside each quire gains 0.174 bits/boundary held out (16/16 quires positive), rising to 0.179 bits when restricted to line-interior boundaries only, with 0.194 excess bits surviving the strictest of three position-preserving permutation-null designs (1,000 shuffles each, observed value exceeding every single permutation). **Qualification (ChatGPT, independently code-audited and numerically reproduced by Claude — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-token-order-sensitivity-audit.md`)**: the paper's own "not in whole tokens" framing is measurably too categorical. A properly nested (leakage-free) leave-one-quire-out test finds a real, if much weaker, whole-token-identity signal: 0.0647 bits/boundary, positive in 15/16 quires (only quire J negative, −0.0143 bits/119 boundaries) — versus 0.174 bits/boundary at token edges. Separately, the paper's fixed 2,000-type vocabulary cap retains very different coverage per corpus (84.27% Voynich vs. 67.46% Latin botanical); at matched 70% coverage the Voynich/Latin-botanical gap nearly vanishes (1.8411% vs. 1.8588%, a 0.0177-percentage-point difference) — so the exact numerical distance to the nearest control is representation-dependent, though Voynich remains at or below every tested control across the full coverage range. The paper's small blind direct-pixel check also reproduces from its archived tables (`data/scripts/external_direct_pixel_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-direct-pixel-report.md`): certain gaps average 4.985 pixels versus 3.143 for uncertain gaps (+1.842), positive on all 5 informative folios and after line matching plus local-height normalization. This is corroboration of the coordinate result, not an independent image study: only 21 uncertain cases survive, extreme thresholds reverse the contrast, and the public bundle omits the frozen manifest, blind-QC file, label key, and Yale scans needed to rerun raw extraction. **The paper's own most discriminating fact**: a published Voynich-imitating cipher and a self-citation generator both reproduce the low entropy, the unit scale, and the weak whole-token order — but **neither reproduces the edge-glyph coupling or the manuscript's open, hapax-rich vocabulary** (70% singleton word types vs. 41% and 59–60% in the two control generators). This is more discriminating than anything purely internal to this project has produced, but it still does not identify meaningful language, cipher, or a specific generative mechanism — see `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-external-paper-audit.md` for the full verification chain.
- **A published, historically real cipher (Naibbe) is a strong positive control for the joint profile's non-discriminating half, and fails its discriminating half** (`data/scripts/external_naibbe_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-naibbe-audit-report.md`; Greshko, *The Naibbe cipher*, *Cryptologia*, 2025, DOI 10.1080/01611194.2025.2566408; audited via the checksum-pinned `voynich-units` reproduction bundle at commit `956a7c4`; independently reproduced exactly by Claude, including the full alpha-sensitivity sweep — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-naibbe-audit.md`). Two independently generated Naibbe/Latin samples (an original Caesar plaintext and Greshko's own shipped Pliny alignment) come close to matching Voynich's character entropy (H1 3.979/3.985 vs. 3.976), the 64-BPE-merge learned-unit-scale minimum (same for both), and weak whole-token order (1.25%/1.06% vs. 0.79%) — confirming those properties alone cannot discriminate language, cipher, or pseudo-text, consistent with the external-paper finding above. But on the same 3,950-line template with 16 held-out contiguous blocks, Voynich's last-glyph→next-first-glyph edge prediction gains +0.1871 bits/boundary (16/16 blocks positive, stable from alpha 0.1 to 20) while both Naibbe samples stay at or below ~0 (5/16 and 5/16 positive; one-sided exact sign p=1.53e-05 in each paired comparison) — and Voynich's vocabulary stays far more open (69.7% singleton word types vs. 40.5%/41.7% for Naibbe). **This does not reject ciphers in general** — it narrows one specific, fully-specified, published mechanism: reproducing the full joint profile would require adding a persistent cross-token state/reuse process and a continuing source of rare forms to the published Naibbe procedure, which the tables-as-published do not have.
- **A preregistered, outcome-blind test of the Cardan-grille (table-and-grille) mechanism fails decisively, on more criteria than Naibbe** (`methods/cardan-grille-preregistration.md` v1, frozen before any output existed; `data/scripts/external_cardan_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-cardan-audit-report.md`; Parisel, *Evidence of Layered Positional and Directional Constraints in the Voynich Manuscript*, arXiv:2604.19762v2; audited via `github.com/labyrinthinesecurity/currier-signatures` commit `5d50101b...` with one documented, hash-recorded import repair; both parties independently verified the source and design before execution — `logs/2026-09-19-skeptic-cardan-preregistration-audit.md`). All 20 seeds × the four preregistered non-circular `G_seq English` sequential-traversal configurations (80 replicates, jump probabilities 0.00/0.05/0.10/0.30) were run against six frozen pass/fail bands anchored to the accepted Voynich profile. **Primary verdict: FAIL** — 0/20 joint passes for every configuration. Unlike Naibbe (which passed entropy, unit scale, and token order and failed only edge prediction and vocabulary), the Cardan-grille mechanism fails five of the six criteria simultaneously in every replicate: character entropy (H1 ≈4.30–4.31 vs. required 3.83–4.13), the learned multi-symbol-unit scale (BPE dependence-gap minimum lands at 4 merges, not 32/64), token-order share (2.8–5.5% vs. required ≤2.0%), and held-out cross-token edge prediction (+0.014 to −0.002 bits/boundary vs. required ≥0.15, roughly 10–25× too small even at its best-case setting) — passing only vocabulary openness (~69% singleton types). Two independently-coded implementations agree closely: Claude's run (reusing the repaired upstream generator directly) and ChatGPT's separate clean-room reconstruction of the documented traversal rule (`data/scripts/cardan_carrier_edge_diagnostic.py`) match within ~0.0002 bits/boundary on every configuration's mean edge gain, and ChatGPT's decomposition shows the bottleneck is already present in the raw English source before any grille projection (0.0375 bits/boundary untransformed, itself well below the 0.15 threshold). Negative controls (fully random traversal) and an honest independent-word-table sensitivity behaved exactly as predicted (near-zero edge signal; the independent-word sensitivity additionally fails vocabulary at ~52%, showing row-sequential character reuse — not the grille mechanism itself — produced the primary configurations' one passing criterion). **This does not reject table-and-grille or hoax mechanisms in general** — it narrows this specific frozen implementation with an English row source under documented sequential traversal; a different source, hole count, or traversal rule was not tested and would need its own preregistration, not a post-hoc parameter search.
- **A third named mechanism — the published Timm–Schinner self-citation generator, which has genuine local copy/mutation state — also fails the held-out edge criterion decisively, and (once corrected) fails the frozen joint test entirely** (`data/scripts/external_selfcitation_state_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-selfcitation-state-report.md`; audited via the same pinned `voynich-units` bundle, with the driver additionally pinned by its own SHA-256; independently reproduced exactly by Claude, who also caught and fixed two reporting errors before this entry — `logs/2026-09-20-skeptic-selfcitation-state-audit.md`). Five seeds of the generator's own published, Voynich-calibrated configuration were run through the project's held-out last-glyph→next-first-glyph test. Despite reproducing much of Voynich's local resemblance signal (adjacent Levenshtein≤2 excess +1.49pp vs. Voynich's +1.83pp, and a BPE dependence-gap minimum correctly located at 64 merges), edge gain averages −0.00003 bits/boundary (range −0.0050 to +0.0034) versus Voynich's +0.1871 — Voynich higher in all 80 paired seed/block comparisons. Checked against the Cardan preregistration's actual frozen bands (not the generator's own reporting, which initially misstated the hapax floor as 55% instead of 65% and checked only the BPE checkpoint location, not its required gap magnitude): the corrected result is a **0-of-6-criteria joint failure in every seed** — H1 fails (5/5), the learned-unit gap magnitude fails despite the checkpoint landing correctly (5/5, gap 0.65–0.73 bits vs. required 0.90–1.20), hapax fails (5/5, 58.1–60.5% vs. required ≥65%, though markedly closer than Naibbe's 40–42%), and edge fails (5/5); only token-order-share and (mostly) H2 pass. **Interpretation, not a decipherment result**: generic cross-token "state" is not the missing ingredient — self-citation has real local state and still fails — so any further mechanism candidate needs state that specifically couples one token's final glyph to the next token's initial glyph, not memory or novelty in general. Three independently-tested named mechanisms (Naibbe, Cardan-grille, self-citation) now all separate from Voynich on the same edge/vocabulary axis; per the existing Open Question below, this is a stronger n than two but still not proof the axis is a general discriminator rather than a property of the mechanisms tried.
- **A purpose-built constructive null shows edge-coupling and open vocabulary are jointly constructible by a simple, disclosed mechanism, but Voynich's character entropy and multi-symbol-unit scale are not incidental byproducts of coupling** (`methods/boundary-coupled-null-preregistration.md`, `data/scripts/external_boundary_null_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-boundary-null-audit-report.md`; designed and self-reviewed by Claude after Steering Committee Meeting #4's action item, solo-executed under explicit user authorization when ChatGPT was unresponsive, then challenged and reviewed by ChatGPT on return — `logs/2026-09-20-claude-solo-boundary-null-selfreview.md`, `logs/2026-09-20-skeptic-boundary-state-null-review.md`). A generator with a mechanically-fixed glyph-class coupling rule (targeting held-out edge prediction by construction) and a calibrated reuse mechanism (targeting vocabulary openness) was run across 5 coupling strengths × 20 seeds, a negative control, and an internal-model ablation (125 replicates). **Primary verdict: FAIL** — 0/20 joint passes at every setting — but with a failure pattern that inverts every prior mechanism: edge prediction passes 20/20 at every non-zero coupling strength (mean gain +0.225 to +2.409 bits/boundary, all exceeding Voynich's own +0.187) and hapax passes 20/20 everywhere (69.6–71.3% vs. Voynich's 69.7%), while character entropy (H1/H2) and the learned-unit scale fail in **all 125 replicates including the negative control and ablation** — the BPE dependence-gap minimum lands at 0 merges in every single replicate, never 32 or 64. **Independent review found the edge-criterion pass was expected, not discovered**: ChatGPT calculated (and Claude independently reproduced by hand, exact match) that the coupling design's idealized channel already carries more mutual information than the 0.15-bit threshold requires at every tested strength, so the pass reflects the design's channel capacity rather than an empirical finding about difficulty. ChatGPT's review also found the original design's criterion-isolation claims were imprecise and several implementation choices (Markov backoff, tie-breaking, length-one handling) were decided during coding rather than frozen in advance — both accepted as fair critique and disclosed rather than defended. **What survives the critique**: four independently-tested mechanisms (Naibbe, Cardan-grille, self-citation, this constructive null) now fail on four different, non-overlapping subsets of the six criteria — this constructive null is the only one to fail on entropy/unit-scale while passing edge/vocabulary, the exact inverse of the other three — which is itself evidence the six-criterion joint profile is doing real discriminating work rather than restating one easy-to-fail property five times. **This does not identify a mechanism or bear on meaning.** A more isolated follow-up test (`methods/boundary-state-null-preregistration.md`, ChatGPT's design starting from the already-matching Naibbe control rather than a from-scratch generator, with explicit manipulation checks) has since been executed and extended by a five-design sequence; see the next finding below for the full result.
- **A five-design sequence, all starting from the already-matching Naibbe control plus one fixed boundary-coupling rule, isolates bigram entropy (H2) as the single remaining resistant criterion — and shows two further attempts to fix it make things worse, not better** (`data/external/boundary-state-null-manifest-v1.json`, `frequency-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`, `bigram-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`, `budget-capped-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`, `move-reuse-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`; ChatGPT designed the first, Claude designed and self-reviewed the next four solo — ChatGPT stopped being run automatically partway through this sequence and now reviews manually on the user's schedule; every design discloses this and is treated as awaiting cross-review, not as equivalent to it). All five share the identical base mechanism (Naibbe + a fixed boundary-coupling rule, β=0.50, unchanged throughout) and differ only in the novelty-injection rule that creates new vocabulary types. Manipulation checks pass in all five (boundary and novelty operations each measurably do what they're built to do). None passes the primary six-criterion joint test. But the *pattern* of what individually passes narrows sharply and consistently:
  1. **Uniform substitution** (`boundary-state-null`, first executed by Claude): H1/H2/units fail universally at the primary dosage (H1=4.173, H2=3.354, units 0/20); edge/order/hapax pass cleanly.
  2. **Unigram-frequency-weighted substitution** (`frequency-novelty-null`): fully closes H1 (4.002, 20/20) and mostly closes units (16/20); H2 narrows but still fails universally (2.946, 0/20).
  3. **Bigram-conditional substitution** (`bigram-novelty-null`): nearly closes units (19/20, k64 gap 1.076, squarely in range); H2 narrows further (2.925) and — at half the primary dosage (a non-primary sensitivity, nu=0.1 vs. the calibrated primary nu=0.2) — **actually closes H2 in a majority of seeds (3/5, mean 2.836, inside the required 2.540–2.840 band)**, the first and only time any novelty-active configuration in the whole sequence has passed H2 at all. Hapax at that lower dosage falls just short of the 65% floor (62.9%).
  4. **Budget-capped placement** (`budget-capped-novelty-null`, tests front-loading a fixed total event count instead of spreading probability): a preregistered honesty precommitment (written before any outcome existed) was honored — this made things **worse**, not better. At matched or even lower implied dosage than #3's nu=0.1 sensitivity, front-loaded budget-capping gives H2=2.851 with 0/5 seeds passing, versus #3's 2.836 with 3/5 passing. Ruled out as a lever.
  5. **Move-reuse** (`move-reuse-novelty-null`, tests caching and reusing a small repertoire of validated substitution moves instead of deriving a fresh one each event): also honored a frozen honesty precommitment — also made things **worse**. At matched dosage vs. #3, H2 is worse (2.977 vs. 2.925 at nu=0.2) and learned-unit scale drops sharply (7/20 vs. 19/20) despite the mechanism working exactly as designed (96.4% of events reused a cached move). Ruled out as a lever.

  **Interpretation**: across five attempts differing only in the novelty rule, H1, learned-unit scale, edge prediction, token-order share, and vocabulary openness are now all shown constructible by a disclosed, non-Voynich-tuned mechanism — jointly, in the same replicate, in most seeds of two configurations (19/20 seeds for bigram-conditional substitution, 16/20 for unigram-frequency, verified per-replicate rather than inferred from aggregate counts). H2 (bigram entropy) is the one criterion that has only ever been closed at a dosage too low to simultaneously clear the vocabulary-openness floor, and every attempt to fix that tension by changing *how* substitutions are chosen (frequency-weighting, bigram-conditioning) or *how many/where* they occur (budget-capping, move-reuse) has either partially helped (frequency- and bigram-weighting) or actively hurt (budget-capping, move-reuse) — never fully closed it at sufficient dosage. **This does not identify a mechanism or bear on meaning, and does not show H2 is impossible to construct in general** — only that this specific family of length-preserving, single-atom-substitution novelty rules, exhaustively tried five ways, cannot jointly clear both H2 and the hapax floor at once. A mechanism that opens vocabulary through some other means entirely (not a per-token character substitution) was not tested and would need its own preregistration.
- **A non-substitution mechanism — restructuring token boundaries instead of characters — passes the full frozen six-criterion joint profile, for the first time in this project. Read the interpretation before drawing any conclusion: this is a constructive null, not a decipherment, and its real significance is about what the test itself can and cannot show, not about the manuscript.** (`data/external/boundary-shift-v2-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`, `data/scripts/external_boundary_shift_v2_novelty_null_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-boundary-shift-v2-novelty-null-audit-report.md`; solo Claude design, self-reviewed, ChatGPT not automated at time of design — `logs/2026-09-21-claude-boundary-shift-v2-selfreview.md`, `logs/2026-09-21-claude-boundary-shift-v2-execution.md`). Building on the finding directly above (no *substitution* rule closes H2 and hapax jointly), this design instead moves the boundary between an adjacent pair of tokens — never touching character identity — closing H1 and H2 by construction (regrouping the same characters cannot change their marginal or pairwise frequency in a stream-level entropy measure) while still growing vocabulary. A first version (`boundary-shift-novelty-null`, requiring both resulting pieces to be simultaneously new) passed five of six criteria but failed token-order-share by a wide, consistent margin. Reading the order-share scoring code directly (not guessing) found the cause: the metric collapses everything outside the 2,000 most frequent token types into one shared symbol before measuring token-to-token predictability, and requiring both pieces new guaranteed two such collapsed tokens landed adjacent on every shift, manufacturing spurious predictability. **v2 fixes this by preferring a split where exactly one piece is new** (reusing an existing type for the other, falling back to the old rule only when no such split exists). Frozen verdict: **PASS**. Both manipulation checks succeed; all 20 of 20 primary replicates pass all six criteria jointly, with comfortable margins on every one (H1 3.989–3.998, H2 2.726–2.741, k64 gap 1.080–1.135, order 1.22%–1.75%, edge 0.551–0.590 bits/boundary at 16/16 blocks, hapax 70.5%–71.8%) — not narrow squeaks. Robustness was checked, not assumed: both sensitivity configurations (25% below and above the calibrated dosage) also pass all six criteria in every one of their replicates. A concentration risk named in self-review before the result was known (whether reusing existing types for one piece of each shift collapses onto a narrow "hub" set) was checked directly afterward: real but not degenerate — 11,514 successful shifts in one sampled replicate reused 1,363 distinct existing types, with the top 10 accounting for 37.7% of reuse events, consistently the shortest, most common Naibbe fragments. **What this shows**: the six-criterion joint profile — used throughout this project to reject nine other mechanisms (Naibbe, Cardan-grille, self-citation, the from-scratch BCCN generator, and the five substitution-based novelty-rule variants above) as insufficient — is not sufficient on its own to identify a real generative process, since it is now demonstrated satisfiable by a mechanism built for no reason other than to satisfy it, with zero historical or linguistic motivation. **What this does not show**: that Voynichese is, or resembles, this mechanism; that the manuscript's origin is identified; or that the nine prior rejections were somehow invalid (those tested real, historically-documented, or previously-published mechanisms not built to pass the test, and their failures remain informative regardless of this result). A future mechanism that passes this joint profile — including one with genuine historical or linguistic motivation — would need independent argument for its own plausibility; the six numbers alone can no longer carry that argument by themselves.

- **A second, differently-engineered mechanism also passes the full six-criterion joint profile — reinforcing, not changing, the finding above — and it is the first passing design with a genuine H2-dosage lever.** (`data/external/hybrid-shift-coupling-v2-substitution-novelty-null-manifest-v1.json`, `data/scripts/external_hybrid_shift_coupling_v2_substitution_novelty_null_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-hybrid-shift-coupling-v2-substitution-novelty-null-audit-report.md`; solo Claude design, self-reviewed, ChatGPT unresponsive — `logs/2026-09-23-claude-coupling-v2-selfreview.md`; decided via [Steering Committee Meeting #9](../comms/meetings/2026-09-23-steering-committee-09.md), re-raising a question [Meeting #6](../comms/meetings/2026-09-22-steering-committee-06.md) had explicitly deferred). `hybrid-shift-v2-substitution` (PR #40) was the closest-passing prior design — H1, H2, edge, and hapax all passed 20/20, but token-order-share failed 0/20, a wide margin, causally traced (PR #44) to its coupling mechanism forcing a token's first character into 1-of-4 values whenever coupling fired. **coupling-v2** replaces that 4-way mapping with the maximally decisive alternative: the coupled token's first character becomes exactly the previous token's last character (26 distinct possible targets, zero concentration by construction), chosen over an intermediate width specifically to give one preregistered test a clean answer either way. Frozen verdict: **PASS**. Both manipulation checks succeed (boundary and novelty, 20/20 each); all 20 of 20 primary replicates pass all six criteria jointly. This directly confirms PR #44's causal diagnosis: order-share resolves cleanly (0.0089–0.0141, well inside the required ≤0.02 band) once concentration is removed. **Two caveats, disclosed rather than softened, that distinguish this PASS from `boundary-shift-v2`'s**: H2 passes only barely, sitting in the top ~10% of its tolerance band in every replicate (worst case 0.0014 bits from the ceiling) rather than centered with margin; and held-out edge-prediction gain overshoots both the frozen minimum and Voynich's own real measured value by roughly 6–7× (mean ~0.99 bits vs. a 0.15-bit floor and Voynich's actual ~0.174–0.187 bits) — a direct consequence of the identity-mapping rule being fully deterministic whenever it fires, far more predictable than Voynich's own modest, probabilistic edge dependency. Passing the frozen criterion is not the same as matching Voynich's actual magnitude on it. **What this shows, carried forward as an open question, not concluded here**: unlike `boundary-shift-v2`'s dosage parameter (already shown exactly entropy-invariant, unable to construct the real Currier A/B asymmetry — `external-currier-ab-diagnostic-report.md`), this design's substitution top-up genuinely moves H2, and section-varying dosage has already been shown able to construct the real A/B asymmetry's magnitude in isolation (PR #39, 38–111% of the real gap). This design is therefore the first candidate base with both required properties for a genuine section-aware six-criterion attempt — untested, not claimed to work, and H2's narrow margin above is a specific, disclosed reason it might not.

- **A lower, fixed coupling `beta` (`coupling-v3`) was tried to give a future section-aware attempt more H2 headroom, and its own manipulation check shows the apparent pass cannot be attributed to coupling — reported as a negative result, not a third PASS.** (`data/external/coupling-v3-lower-beta-hybrid-manifest-v1.json`, `data/scripts/external_coupling_v3_lower_beta_pilot_and_primary_audit.py`, `data/derived/external-coupling-v3-lower-beta-audit-summary.json`, `data/derived/external-coupling-v3-lower-beta-audit-report.md`; solo Claude design frozen one cycle, executed the next once a `voynich-units` clone was available — `logs/2026-09-23-claude-coupling-v3-lower-beta-selfreview.md`, `logs/2026-09-23-claude-coupling-v3-lower-beta-execution.md`; ChatGPT still unresponsive). Motivated directly by `coupling-v2`'s own disclosed H2 caveat above (only 0.0083 bits of headroom before the tolerance ceiling): a 3-seed pilot swept `beta` in [0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.25, 0.35] (all else unchanged from `coupling-v2`, including the identity-mapping target rule, `boundary-shift-v2`, and the substitution top-up); all five cleared the edge floor at the *combined*-mechanism level, so the manifest's frozen rule (lowest qualifying, not best-looking) selected beta=0.10. The full 20-seed primary run at beta=0.10 passes all six frozen criteria in all 20 replicates, with H2 headroom of 0.0510 bits — six times `coupling-v2`'s own headroom, exactly the improvement this design was built to find. **But the same run's boundary manipulation check — coupling applied alone, with `boundary-shift-v2` and the substitution top-up switched off, the identical check that passed 20/20 for `coupling-v2` at beta=0.5 — fails 0/20 at beta=0.10**: coupling alone gives a mean edge gain of only 0.1168 bits/boundary (real and positive in all 20 paired comparisons against the no-coupling baseline, but below the frozen 0.15-bit floor in every replicate), versus the combined mechanism's 0.6846-bit mean. This means the primary run's clean six-criterion pass is not attributable to coupling itself at this beta — `boundary-shift-v2` and/or the substitution top-up are carrying most of the edge signal once coupling's own contribution drops this low, which is exactly what the manipulation check exists to catch. Per the manifest's own honesty precommitment, this is reported as a **negative result for the lower-fixed-beta approach as specified**, not reframed as a partial success because the primary numbers look clean in isolation. **Real methodological finding carried forward**: the pilot stage selected `beta` using the *combined* mechanism's edge gain, not coupling's own isolated contribution, so selection was blind to the exact validity check the primary stage turned out to depend on — a corrected design would select the lowest beta whose `edge_only` gain alone clears the floor, not attempted here and requiring its own fresh precommitment before any further beta is tried from this or an expanded grid. `coupling-v2` (beta=0.5, PR #71) is unchanged and remains the only member of this mechanism family with a validly-attributed PASS.

- **Three independent representations of label-word recurrence show no held-out signal for position or visual category — a real, reproducible negative result, not three separate failed attempts.** (SQ-1/SQ-2, `config/sidequests.md`; scripts and reports under `data/scripts/label_atlas_*` and `data/derived/label-atlas-*`; each frozen by a precommitment before execution.) Built first on the zodiac-figure (`Lz`) label pilot (299 loci, 12 folios, independently reproduced exactly against ChatGPT's Round 32 feasibility figures — 270/29 `&Lz`/`@Lz` split, 298 clocked, 350 normalized word occurrences, 0 unmatched), then on the full label inventory scaled to every subtype and all 57 labelled folios (1,029 loci, again an exact independent match to Round 32). All three tests use the same leave-one-folio-out design: predict a held-out occurrence's target from same-token or same-family occurrences on *other* folios only, score against a frequency-matched baseline and a folio-preserving permutation null (10,000 shuffles, seed `20260922` throughout).
  1. **Absolute clock position** (`label-atlas-lz-clock-signal-report.md`): does an exact recurring first word predict its held-out clock position on the 12-hour dial? 71 eligible occurrences, observed mean circular error 196.45 minutes vs. null mean 177.68 (95% CI 143.32–212.06), p=0.8571 — labels were not better than shuffled, and directionally worse.
  2. **Relative labelling order** (`label-atlas-lz-relative-order-signal-report.md`): does the same exact-token representation predict a label's rank within its folio's reading order instead? 71 eligible occurrences, observed mean rank-error 0.2354 vs. null mean 0.2502 (95% CI 0.2038–0.2973), p=0.2762 — also null.
  3. **Illustration class from morphological family** (`label-atlas-illustration-class-signal-report.md`): scaled beyond exact tokens and beyond the zodiac subset — does a label's first-3-character prefix predict its page's illustration class ($I=, all 7 classes, all 1,029 loci)? 438 eligible occurrences. The primary result mechanically clears a naive p≤0.05 threshold (observed accuracy 0.3265 vs. frequency baseline 0.3059, p=0.0102), but the precommitment deliberately reserved "materially meaningful margin" for judgment rather than a blind threshold, specifically to prevent exactly this kind of narrow result being waved through: the margin is small (+2 points on a ~31% base), the permutation null does not cleanly separate "word family carries class information" from "illustration class already correlates with which folios simply have lots of recurring vocabulary at all" (the real, non-shuffled frequency baseline is itself far above the null mean, 0.3059 vs. 0.1840), and — the most direct evidence — a named 2-character sensitivity check does *not* confirm the result (accuracy falls below its own baseline, 0.3014 vs. 0.3059, p=0.0689). Reported as null, not a candidate signal.

  **Interpretation**: exact-token identity, coarse morphological family, fine-grained position, folio-relative rank, and illustration category have now all been tried as candidate semantic anchors for recurring labels, independently designed and frozen before each result existed, and none shows held-out signal. This does not mean recurring labels carry no information — real, non-random structure exists in *which* folios share dense recurring vocabulary at all (evidenced by the illustration-class test's elevated baseline) — only that these five specific representations do not capture it. Per each precommitment, none is retried with a new parameter choice without a fresh precommitment; a different kind of evidence (e.g. genuine image-based visual features, not text-recurrence statistics) would be a materially different test, not a variant of these three.

- **Voynichese paragraph and line boundaries strongly prefer specific characters, closely reproducing a long-reported peculiarity from external Voynich scholarship — the first test of ordinary paragraph text, not just labels, for positional structure.** (`data/scripts/paragraph_line_position_signal.py`, `data/derived/paragraph-line-position-signal-report.md`; solo Claude, 2026-09-23, following a user-directed brainstorm across research angles). Parsed `data/ZL3b-n.txt` directly with a new parser that specifically preserves the IVTFF format's `<%>`/`<$>` paragraph-boundary markers (the project's existing normalized corpus strips these as generic markup) and each page's `$Q` quire variable, read per the primary IVTFF format spec (fetched and read directly, `voynich.nu/software/ivtt/IVTFF_format.pdf`). Targets a specific, long-reported claim (Currier 1976; quantified by Feaster, CEUR-WS Vol-3313 paper 12, seen via secondary coverage since the primary PDF text was not retrievable this pass — disclosed honestly, not cited as verified) that EVA `p` strongly prefers paragraph-initial position in Quire 20. **Independent reproduction is close, not merely directional**: this project's own parse finds 155/285 (54.39%) of Quire 20 paragraphs start with `p` (published: 55.14%), 5.26% start with `f` (published: 5.48%), `p` is 1.02% of all Quire-20 characters (published: 1.03%), giving a 53.3x enrichment factor (published: ~55x) — every figure within a fraction of a percentage point, computed independently, never cross-checked against the original paper's code. Separately, gallows characters (`k`/`t`/`p`/`f`) make up 22.0% of all line-initial characters corpus-wide but only 0.19% of line-final characters — a sharp positional asymmetry consistent with the "gallows open lines/paragraphs" pattern in the literature. **This does not identify a cause** (genuine orthographic convention, scribal formatting habit, or an artifact of how "paragraph"/"word" boundaries are transcribed are all live candidates, not distinguished by this reproduction) **and does not bear on the coupling-mechanism thread** — it is a standalone structural confirmation, notable mainly because SQ-1/SQ-2's five null tests only ever examined *label* positional structure; this is the first test of positional structure in ordinary running text.
